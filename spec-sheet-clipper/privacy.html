Spec Sheet Clipper privacy policy

Effective November 23, 2026. Applies to version 1.0.0.

What the extension reads

When you open the clipper on a webpage, it reads that page's title, URL and the text you've selected. You can also enter a product name, room, model, finish, notes and a project name.

Where the data goes

Saved selections and the project name are stored in chrome.storage.local in your Chrome profile. The extension doesn't send them to NSS Home or any other server. It has no analytics, tracking, advertising, remote code or account system. It doesn't use Chrome sync storage.

Permissions

activeTab provides temporary access to the page you invoke the extension on. scripting reads the page title, URL and selected text. storage saves your selections and project name locally.

Exports and links

CSV export creates a file on your computer. Printing uses the browser's print dialog, including its Save as PDF option. If you open a product or NSS website link, your browser contacts that site under its own privacy practices. Where you choose to save or share an export is outside this extension.

Removal and retention

Use Remove selection to delete an item. Undo can restore the most recently removed item while that sheet page remains open. Data otherwise remains in your Chrome profile until you remove it or uninstall the extension. Export a copy before uninstalling if you want to keep your selections.
