Whole House Remodel Checklist Tacoma

Whole house remodel checklist Tacoma: record each room’s problems, priorities, and decisions in one place.

Use this free guide before your first design meeting. Check items as you go, add notes, and print a copy. Your entries stay in this browser.

01 / Define what needs to change

Write down the problems you want solved. Separate a necessary change from a preference. Walk the house in the order you use it.

02 / Build a room-by-room scope

Include connecting spaces. A kitchen change can affect adjacent flooring, lighting, structure and circulation.

03 / Make proposals comparable

Ask each firm to explain the same scope. Keep open decisions visible so an allowance doesn't become an assumed final selection.

04 / Plan daily life during construction

Ask which areas can stay occupied and when. The answer depends on your house and the work.

05 / Bring evidence to the first meeting

Collect available plans, photos, your room list and any product specifications. Confirm the project address, permitting jurisdiction and any property-specific review before finalizing work.
